Case Law for the Street

Quick-reference case law for officers — please read this first.

This app is a quick-reference educational tool published by Mak Around Original. It summarizes case law in plain language for general information only. It is NOT legal advice, does not create an attorney-client relationship, and is not a substitute for your agency's policy, your state law, or guidance from your agency's legal advisor or prosecutor. Case law varies by state and federal circuit and changes over time. Your agency policy may be stricter than the law — policy controls your conduct. Every card shows the date it was last verified; verify critical decisions against the full opinion and current law. Nothing in this app overrides officer safety or your judgment in a rapidly evolving situation.

Put it on your home screen

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen / Install app.

iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Open it once while you have signal and it saves a copy on this device — after that it opens and searches with no signal at all. When you’re back online, it picks up corrections and new cards on its own.

Cards state what the courts held. They do not direct your decision on any stop — agency policy and the facts in front of you control.
